Get started

Install OpenYap

The app needs macOS 26 and an Apple silicon Mac.

  1. 1

    Download and open the ZIP file.

  2. 2

    Move OpenYap.app to the Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Open OpenYap and grant Microphone and Input Monitoring access.

Grant Accessibility access if you want OpenYap to paste text into other apps.

Your voice stays yours.

OpenYap does not retain microphone audio. Transcript history, vocabulary, snippets, and statistics stay local.
